## Quick reference

**Brand name:** Lotsy · **Tagline:** Tanie weekendy z Polski · **Language:** Polish (pl-PL)

**Key colors:**
- Page background: `#f6f6f6`
- Cards/surfaces: `#ffffff`
- Primary text: `#1a1a1a` · Secondary: `#6b7280` · Tertiary: `#9ca3af`
- Borders/dividers: `#e5e7eb`
- Orange (logo only): `#f48120`
- Links/CTA/active: `#1d4ed8` (blue-700)
- Active row border: `#2563eb` (3px left border)
- Success/good-deal: `#16a34a`
- Badge bg: `#dbeafe` · Badge text: `#1e40af`

**Font:** Outfit (Google Fonts) — `wght@400;500;600;700;800`

**Components available:** Button, Badge, Card, Input, DealCard, RouteChip

**Tone:** Informal ty-form Polish · friendly, direct, deal-savvy · no emoji · sentence case

**Key copy patterns:**
- CTA: "Sprawdź" (not "Kup" / "Rezerwuj")
- Price: "od 299 PLN"
- Route: "WAW → BCN"
- Good deal: "✓ Dobra okazja"
